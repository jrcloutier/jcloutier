Deadly Dust

Pittsburgh Post-Gazette

A two-part investigation into the federal government's failure to protect coal miners from deadly silica and coal dust. Reporting revealed how the Mine Safety and Health Administration missed hundreds of inspections and failed to enforce federal exposure limits, leaving thousands of miners across the country breathing hazardous levels of toxic dust — fueling a devastating resurgence of black lung disease.
