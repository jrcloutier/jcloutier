Pennsylvania is seeing its worst measles surge since 1991
State health officials have confirmed 60 measles cases in two separate outbreaks in Pennsylvania this year — the highest number in more than three decades. The current outbreak started in late April and has infected 48 people so far. The other began in February and ended weeks later after impacting a dozen people. The majority of patients affected in both surges were school-aged children. The cases come as tens of thousands of parents are now opting to skip the shots for their children, driving down immunization rates in hundreds of schools across the state, the Post-Gazette found.
