More than 100 measles cases confirmed in Pa.

The Pennsylvania Department of Health has confirmed 101 cases across eight counties in 2026 — the highest number in more than three decades. The current outbreak, centered in Lancaster and Lebanon counties, has totaled 89 cases so far and began in late April. An earlier, separate outbreak that started in Lancaster County in January infected a dozen people before it was contained.
